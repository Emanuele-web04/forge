## What revision 2 changes in the verdict

The earlier local shortcomings in Space cancellation and exposed semantic no-replay are now addressed. Cua verifies exact PID/window, visibility, Space and optional observed bounds at admission and before further native posts; Space changes invalidate the active operation without blocking its matching releases. Exposed AXPress/selection/set-value paths choose an actuator before writing and stop after an uncertain write. Forced synthetic typing retains its original single-route contract. Initial refusal and possible partial effects remain distinct.

This incorporates useful Swift ideas while preserving Cua's stronger shutdown design: native held-input and whole-action ownership, authenticated exact-PID cleanup acknowledgement, and replacement only after cleanup plus old-process exit. Independent review found and corrected missing readiness authorization and unguarded foreground activation boundaries before the final native build. Final gate closures and cleanup paths were re-reviewed with no outstanding finding.

- The candidate's `Tests/README.md:64` explicitly says the performance refresh was developed without macOS native compilation/timing evidence. The prior review found no current-head native verification establishing an equivalent comparison. This is missing evidence, not proof Swift fails to build.

Fresh signed-app revision 2 actuation, provider-to-desktop qualification, long RAM/CPU runs and mixed-display behavior remain unqualified. No new native execution was performed in this final comparative review. Full workspace checks and any resulting TypeScript fixes are owned by the parent task.

The next native work worth adapting is pixel/modified scroll, real hover, background drag, richer menu/reveal semantics and a smaller cursor representation. These should be treated as separately tested capability work inside the single backend, not silently claimed as already imported. A wholesale Swift switch would trade away established cleanup/effect protections while still requiring native qualification and correction of the candidate findings.
